03 / Method

Simple enough to trust.

1

Segment

Clean the local corpus, detect seven book boundaries, and preserve paragraph-sized evidence windows.

2

Recognise

Resolve aliases with a typed, auditable gazetteer and strict word boundaries.

3

Enrich

Connect repeated co-occurrences and average VADER compound sentiment over their evidence paragraphs.

4

Interrogate

Calculate degree and Louvain communities, then explore filters, hop distance and shortest paths.

Transparent by design. Every weight can be reproduced from the local corpus; limitations and evaluation are documented in the README.
